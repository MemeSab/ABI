Strive Podcast

Real-life stories of strength, marriage, wellness, and the ability to keep striving through life’s challenges.

In Episode 2 of the Strive Podcast, Abigail is joined by her sixteen-year-old daughter Lola for an honest conversation about what life actually feels like at sixteen — from the pressure to always be online to the standards nobody can reach.

They talk about comparison and friendship, faith and rest, and the difference between wanting to grow and believing you are not enough.

Welcome to an exclusive first listen of the Strive Podcast. In this special pilot conversation, Abigail is joined by her husband Graeme (married 28 years!) for an unscripted, humorous, and heartfelt Q&A.

From parenting in the chaos and navigating perimenopause (“like a cactus”), to rediscovering self-worth and choosing to want each other every single day, this 10-minute highlight gives you a true taste of what Strive is all about.
